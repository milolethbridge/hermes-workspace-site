Hermes Workspace

A private, single-user automation workspace operated by Milo Sheppard.

Hermes Workspace is a personal assistant environment that connects to the owner's own Google account so it can read and organise his email, files and calendar on his behalf. It is not offered as a service to the public and has exactly one user: the account owner.

What it does

  • Reads and searches Gmail messages, and sends mail from the owner's mailbox.
  • Reads and organises files in the owner's Google Drive.
  • Reads and edits the owner's Google Calendar events.
  • Reads and writes the owner's Google Sheets spreadsheets.
